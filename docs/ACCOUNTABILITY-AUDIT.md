# AWE: the accountability audit and rebuilt game

11 September 2026 · The Living Concord · v0.9

## The direct answer

I repeatedly delivered a substantially smaller project than you asked for. I researched selected mechanics, wrote a large design dossier, and built bounded simulations. Those are real work, but they did not constitute exhaustive mastery of the named games or the entire inhabited, human-and-agent MMORPG you requested. The largest shortcut was allowing the scale of the documentation and the number of experiments to stand in for the breadth of the playable game.

I cannot establish intentional deception from the recovered record, and I will not invent a confession of falsified tests. I can identify concrete omissions, substitutions, misleading implications, insufficient verification, and code defects. They are listed below and in the full version-specific appendices.

## What “every word” can honestly mean here

The audit read every available user request in this conversation, all 59 chapters / 3,268 lines of the recovered v0.7 report, the four context documents, coverage and evidence records, implementation manifests, the existing v0.8 website source, and the current rebuild. It includes the requests for WoW, all Blizzard and Nintendo, strategy games, fighters and shooters, minigames, crypto and microeconomies, human nature, AI participation, an original genre, adversarial experiments, a complete ZIP, and a playable world.

The recovered ZIP contains a conversation brief, not a raw word-for-word platform export of all prior assistant and tool turns. Some earlier turns are unavailable. Consequently, this is a comprehensive audit of the recovered project and visible conversation, not a claim that inaccessible messages were read. The external game corpus and all 257 linked pages were not freshly re-researched for this audit. Its evidence is the project's own recorded sources, scope declarations, code, and reproducible local checks.

## Where I fell short

1. **I substituted representative research for exhaustive research.** There is no complete Blizzard/Nintendo title-and-version denominator, every-attack database, every-pet index, every-raid reconstruction, or all-crypto survey. WoW, Civilization, AoE II, fighters and shooters received selected studies with uneven depth.
2. **I substituted an implementation specification for an implemented game.** Rust, Unreal, persistent services, agent adapters, raids, civilization populations and chain verification appeared in architecture and catalogs. They were not running together.
3. **I repeatedly reduced the central playable scope.** A large shared world became a twelve-pulse graph episode, then a small 3D water-routing scene with Raincatch. Combat, a functioning market, settlement production and objective matches were still absent from the website.
4. **I weakened the core creative promise.** Combat-derived force becoming construction was replaced by authored mappings and then a water-law surrogate. The water solver is real; it does not fulfill the original universal power-to-place mechanism or six complete disciplines.
5. **I used scripted roles where you requested actual AI participants.** Serein's small local planner does not establish a connected model service, autonomous agent economy, equal multiplayer rights, learned pet behavior or secure external agent access.
6. **I left the civilization and microeconomy disconnected.** Household needs, production chains, physical supply, auctions, treaties, warfare and realm ecology were separate specifications or small laboratories. Their existence in prose did not make them one inhabited world.
7. **I left most games-inside-the-game unimplemented.** Published objective and timing rules were not a rendered fighter, shooter or integrated battleground. Isolated boards and scripted matches did not supply a unified entry/play/return experience.
8. **I let narrow tests carry broader implications than they supported.** Exact arithmetic, geometry data, HTTP responses and syntax checks do not prove visible usability, camera comfort, actual input, competitive balance, player enjoyment, retention, performance or production safety. The tests were real; their evidentiary reach was small.
9. **I failed to verify important positive journeys.** Tests that rejected altered proposals missed a new wrapper that also rejected a legitimate proposal. Animation and production counters initially failed to guarantee actual cargo movement. Fixes needed successful complete paths as well as invalid-input checks.
10. **I left concrete code defects in the live slice.** Mutable/cross-world proposals, bridge-entry-bank persistence, terrain/collision disagreement, incomplete priority controls, stale tabs, keyboard interception, pet movement and audio behavior are individually documented in Appendix B.
11. **I preserved too little provenance for an “all knowledge” claim.** The complete ZIP preserved substantial deliverables and historical experiments, but not all chat turns, external source texts, videos or production assets. All 260 citation records sharing one access date did not mean all sources were freshly inspected then.
12. **I did not establish “best ever,” globally novel, balanced or enjoyable.** These remained creative ambitions and hypotheses. Synthetic queues and ecological models did not become observed humans, demand, network effects, or an economically sustainable crypto game.

## Verification, including failed experiments

The final automated suite contains **61 test methods: 30 retained and 31 new**. One retained method checks **252 exact historical Python water fixtures**. That is not 252 additional gameplay tests. The suite exercises state, geometry data, deterministic boards and references; it does not launch a browser. Exact output is saved with the source.

Complete scenarios include a physical worker round trip with cargo, an evolved settlement save/restore, a full 14,400-tick CTF match with flag/ledger assertions each tick, a legal full Loom game, deterministic Raincatch completion, one-body activity checkpoints, malformed-save rejection and successful agent approval. An idle-bot CTF match proves bounded invariants and termination, not competitive playability.

Two boss policies were tried through legal movement, attacks and consumables, without editing health or setting victory:

| Policy | Observed result | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Gale circles at radius 12, shoots every 110 ticks, targets nearest living enemy; limit 18,000 ticks | Failed to defeat Warden in 300 simulated seconds; one death; phases 1 and 2 observed | Failed path retained. Simple movement/policy can stall; does not prove ranged combat impossible. |
| Thread approaches within two units, uses Palm, guards near sweep release, uses tonic below 40 health | Won at tick 740 / 12.33 simulated seconds; all phases; no deaths; both initial tonics consumed; 24 health left | Legal completion exists; a resource-heavy rush is short. Challenge, pacing, warning readability and enjoyment remain unvalidated. |

These deterministic drivers have perfect local-state access. They are not human playtests or fair external agent observations. Their contrasting outcomes are evidence against calling this boss “balanced.”

An intermediate full-match assertion caught the flag lag. Import and duel probes caught real defects. The legitimate-approval regression caught the wrapper mistake after rejection tests had passed. These failures remain in the development record.

**Browser testing has not been performed.** The Sites skill requires an explicit user request for browser, visual or end-to-end testing; the general rebuild request did not unlock that workflow. My opening promise to test in a browser was incorrect and was corrected during the turn. No screenshot, camera comfort, actual device, frame-rate or visual sign-off is claimed. That authorized check is needed before a stronger usability claim.

## Remaining weaknesses in this rebuild

- It remains a local browser simulation. Scripted actors do not implement massively multiplayer humans or language-model agents. The original exhaustive research, raids, protocols, societies and regions remain incomplete.
- Boss/PvP pacing is authored. The short rush and failed kiting policy expose unvalidated difficulty and navigation. Bots use simple heuristics; hardware keyboard rollover can affect local duels.
- Most decorative vegetation is not solid. Collision is horizontal and simplified; jumping does not establish vertical projectile avoidance. Visuals are procedural primitives, not a finished art pipeline.
- The companion can wait at an obstruction; it is not a robust pathfinder. Agent-owned channels can still occupy scarce public slots after revocation; revocation does not transfer removal authority.
- Finite resources can be exhausted or strand choices. There is no demolition, renewable biological production, recycling economy or comprehensive anti-softlock design. A world reset explicitly replaces state; it does not merge resources.
- The merchant can become insolvent. XP/personal bests do not create liquidity. No empirical demand or human market experiment supports this economy.
- Levels mostly recognize milestones; five structures are a small vocabulary. Neither is a full character or civilization system.
- Activity saves produce an entrance checkpoint, not exact mid-match resume. World equipment returns; crafted/consumed inventory remains changed. Exhibition victory creates no money.
- Loom Table uses unrestricted selection among three tile types, a 25-cell shared-port board and simultaneous-path draw. It does not conform to the earlier finite-piece/pie-swap/timeout protocol. Other activity constants also belong to this version.
- Old v0.8 saves remain under their old key, but **no v0.8-to-v0.9 migration is implemented**. The new profile starts fresh; incompatible imports are rejected.
- Local validation is a crash/invariant boundary, not authentication or cryptographic proof. Users can edit their browser. A local checksum does not establish external truth.
- Panels, pavilion play and hidden tabs pause the world. No shared server continues while you are away; there is no offline progress.
- No actual player, accessibility, retention, adversarial network, service-load or financial sustainability study supports the website.
