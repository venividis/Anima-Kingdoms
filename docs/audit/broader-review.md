# Anima Kingdoms: independent broader-project audit

12 September 2026. Review of the incoming Anima Kingdoms source and the current release work. This is a finite registry of **36 distinct unresolved obligation families: 27 game/engineering families and nine research families**. It is not a list of 36 newly discovered bugs or a statement that all 36 can be completed in this release.

The historical dossier's 119 finding labels include overlapping scope reductions, old corrected defects and evidence caveats. Counting them as 119 current defects would misrepresent both the remaining work and the substantial working game. The registry below merges repeated descriptions while retaining their actual acceptance requirements. A narrow implemented gate changes that gate's status; it does not close the entire original ambition.

## Review boundary

Reviewed `ACCOUNTABILITY-AUDIT.md`, `WHOLE-GAME-PLAN.md`, `audit/dossier-audit.md`, `ANIMA-KINGDOMS.md`, the current realm, connection, water, navigation and combat paths, selected creation and persistence tests, and the prior connected-core audit. The recovered shared transcript explicitly asks for agents to play through controls and as agents. The probes in this review use legal core functions and an invited command policy. They do not constitute browser interaction or human playtesting.

No exhaustive new commercial-game research, external source refresh, onchain deployment, live language-model service, service-load measurement or human study was performed by this reviewer. The implemented civilization, authenticated Commons and UI changes carry their current closure statement in [the release contract](../BROADER-FOUNDATION.md). This registry records the incoming source's gaps and retains the larger obligations beyond those bounded gates.

## What is already implemented and must not be called absent again

- An embodied 3D valley and a same-simulation canvas fallback, walkable authored structures, collision, activities, four timed base attacks, guard, evade, projectiles, a local boss, bot CTF and local two-person duels exist. The later combat patch adds committed aim, buffering, touch/keyboard helpers and practice.
- The five creation kinds are real bounded interfaces: creatures, relics, structures, instruments and sigil trials. Drafting, experiments/rehearsal, immutable instances, import/export, paid placement and safe reclamation exist. Building demolition also exists; the v0.9 text saying there is none is historical.
- Workers carry actual goods. Finite merchant transactions conserve goods and money. A fixed civic courier request now physically picks up, transports and consumes two stone and transfers four reserved Marks exactly once.
- Song events now send paid, pitch-filtered packets to selected receivers. Breaking a connection or exhausting charge has real mechanical effects. The proposed v0.10 “song that changes a place” is therefore implemented in a bounded form. Ordered melody recognition, arbitrary relay circuits and general acoustic physics are separate unimplemented claims.
- Snapshots, compatibility validation, local save ownership, retained recovery exports and finite command receipts exist. Those do not establish authenticated shared server authority.
- Prior legal tests exercise positive journeys, malformed saves, command replay, revoked grants, physical cargo, activity return, reclamation and musical charge conservation. Their presence does not eliminate the need for actual browser/device and hostile-client observations.

## Three release gates that should change the project substantially

1. **A renewable inhabited loop:** construct a producer, supply explicit inputs, grow within carrying capacity, send a physical carrier, unload once, consume at households, enter shortage when supply fails, recover through another legal route, save/reload mid-cargo and continue. The ledger must distinguish genuine modeled production from initial reserves and show where consumed goods went. This advances C09–C12 together through one observed causal journey.
2. **A real shared exchange:** two authenticated principals obtain service-owned finite assets, create an offer, race two accepts, interrupt/restart and recover exactly one trade. A third principal and a revoked controller fail without mutation. This advances the narrow C08/C16/C25/C26 gates; it does not close C01 shared embodied-world gameplay.
3. **Usable connected controls:** start fresh, walk to the exchange, buy actual material, author/place or use the guided kit, fuel/feed, complete a delivery and return, inspect a shortage/blocked route, recover, then export/reload. Actually observe the browser journey and preserve its failures. This advances C24 and verifies integrations; it does not prove aesthetic excellence or human enjoyment.

If a deliverable is only a separate service panel, its connection to the game must be honest: server custody and local materials are different authorities. Never upload an editable local pack and trust it as a deposit into the shared exchange. If shared assets do not yet alter the valley, say exactly what is shared.

## Adversarial findings and repairs from this review

### B01: guided walking bypassed guard and attack movement penalties

From an untouched new realm, the agent requested `walk` to `(0, 0)` and held guard for ten ticks. Guided travel moved **0.7166667** units; manual guarded travel moved **0.3225**, a **2.2222×** advantage. Holding Palm moved the same **0.7166667** guided units versus **0.3941667** manually, a **1.8182×** advantage. No position, inventory, health or world field was edited to set up these cases.

Cause: ordinary actor input applies guard and action movement multipliers, but `Kingdoms.tick` called the navigation steering helper at an unconditional 4.3 units/second. It cancelled on discrete attack input, not held attack, guard or an already committed action. Navigation could also change the body's guard-facing direction after input.

**Repaired:** guided walking now yields to manual movement, guard, discrete/held attacks, a committed action/evade and jump/airborne movement. It does not add a second movement step. Ordinary unobstructed walking still moves at 4.3 units/second and reaches a legal destination. This changes route cancellation behavior intentionally; it does not alter base attack timing.

### B02: revocation rejected new agent commands but left the accepted walk running

The agent received a grant, requested a walk and advanced one tick. After human revocation, the same body moved another **4.3 units in 60 ticks**. The journey stored no controller identity. The prior text promised epoch rejection for future commands; it did not explicitly promise cancellation of all previously accepted jobs. This was a control-contract gap, not evidence that the existing revoked-epoch validation was bypassed.

**Strengthened behavior:** disabling the connection grant stops any queued local journey, including a human-started one. This deliberate rule is safe for a single shared body and avoids guessing who owns the pending movement. It does not reclaim placed creations, delete wires, cancel cargo custody or reverse completed actions. Stale agent requests still reject without mutation, and the stopped journey remains stopped after snapshot/restore.

### B03 / H37: public water-slot capture repaired prospectively; legacy rights retained

Through the exported legal water API, an invited agent used eight of its twelve existing fiber to place a capacity-one installed channel in each of the eight valid slots. After revocation, all eight `Rain.removeChannel` calls rejected with “Only your own channels can be salvaged.” Installing the Mercy Braid also rejected because its slots were occupied. After twenty legal rain pulses, the bridge retained only four water and the orchard/reeds were empty. Money, water, fiber and focus ledgers remained valid; the stranded world saved and reloaded successfully.

This is a **known historical unresolved requirement**, reproduced against the incoming current source, not a new defect discovered by counting an old label. Valid accounting and revoked future authority do not provide infrastructure recovery. The typed Serein preview does not deliberately propose this particular eight-slot attack, so this case does not claim that the ordinary proposal button will necessarily produce it.

The incoming removal API was `Rain.removeChannel(world, key)`, restricted to `owner === 'human'`. It had no agent-owner removal argument or previously accepted caretaker rights.

**Prospective repair implemented:** every newly built agent channel now records `owner: 'agent'` and `maintainer: 'human'`. The human may maintain a marked channel after invitation revocation. For an installed channel, `floor(capacity / 2)` returns only to the agent's fiber account; the remainder becomes scrap. Temporary-channel removal refunds neither spent Focus nor fiber. Stored water is unaffected. The new invitation and installation events state these terms. Validation permits the marker only with that exact human-maintainer/agent-owner combination, and a maintenance event invalidates an already viewed proposal through the existing revision check.

**Historical limit retained:** an existing agent channel without the marker remains owner-only through reload, revocation and re-invitation. No automatic migration inserts caretaker rights into historical property. Thus the future eight-slot trap is repaired, while an already stranded legacy save still needs an explicitly accepted repair arrangement or another route. This local property rule does not establish online governance or secure authentication against edited saves.

Eight new stewardship tests cover all-slot recovery, odd-capacity salvage custody, repeated-removal rejection, unmodified human behavior, legacy preservation, malformed ownership/marker rejection, temporary removal/expiry and stale proposals. The positive sequence fills all eight slots under a real grant, revokes, clears them, rebuilds a human-funded Mercy Braid, runs the physical wagon to its destination and reloads successfully. All money, water, fiber and Focus ledgers remain conserved. The corresponding output is retained in `broader/stewardship-tests.txt`.

The final related run passed **105 methods** across water, stewardship, realm integration, persistence, guided control and connected creation. It includes the previously reported 33 connected/guided methods; do not add 33 to 105. Its historical ownership test now explicitly loads an unmarked legacy-channel fixture. Command: `node --test tests/world.test.mjs tests/water-stewardship.test.mjs tests/concord.test.mjs tests/kingdoms-persistence.test.mjs tests/guided-authority.test.mjs tests/kingdoms-connections.test.mjs`.

## Legal invited-agent positive journey

The independent policy starts at the untouched normal spawn. The human gives one 20-command grant; the policy performs **nine agent commands**, with no fixture teleportation, inventory injection, forced victories or health changes:

1. Walk to Vey's actual exchange and buy one crystal through `buy_crystal`.
2. Walk to the bridgehead; instantiate the ordinary three-creation guided kit.
3. Fuel the instrument, feed the courier, perform the score and assign the real two-stone request.
4. Let the courier physically perform both deliveries. Snapshot/restore at six custody/phase transitions.
5. Perform again after payment so the courier has a powered route home; continue until its job ends.

The pre-repair and post-repair source both complete this existing positive path at **tick 2666**, leaving **11 grant commands**. Two stone were consumed, the four-Mark reward paid once, and the courier returned. All six material residuals were zero; total money remained 50. Charge ended as **24 issued = 8 at source + 0 in flight + 4 at receiver + 12 spent + 0 dissipated**. This confirms the existing connected invention is real and remained working after the movement/revocation changes.

The policy reads complete local state and is deterministic. It is not a connected model inside the product, a fair hidden-information competitive agent, mouse/touch interaction or a human playtest.
