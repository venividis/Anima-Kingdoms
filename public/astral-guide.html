Follow your silver thread.

38 worlds. 91 places to enter. A solar pilgrimage in the language of Luma.

In the game, choose Astral travel.

Let your soul rise from your resting body, then look into the whole night sky. Touch a planet to choose that world or its moons. Follow Asteroid paths for the smaller worlds. Drag to look around, use Through Earth to see the other hemisphere, or choose Just the stars for a quiet view. At your destination, drag to orbit; pinch or scroll to approach. Choose a landmark, then Enter this place. Fly with WASD or the arrow keys; Q/E lower and raise you. On touchscreens, use the flight pads and drag to look.

Listen follows Luma's original letter pitches. Encounter lets you save a reflection after entering a place. Night sky returns you to the stars. Return to body follows your thread home. Your town rests while you travel.

How these worlds are made

The night sky reuses Luma’s 9,096 catalogued stars and all 88 constellation regions. It preserves your town’s observer and clock, while astral sight reveals stars even in daylight. Planet lights use the existing calculated sky positions; the asteroid gate and moon paths are authored navigation.

These are authored astral landscapes inspired by real places. The globe maps use planetary imagery where available; local terrain, weather, colors, companion positions and Luma associations are artistic interpretations. Local scenes are finite landscapes, with flight bounds. Sizes and travel time are scaled for exploration. Giant-planet destinations are cloud flights; the Sun is a plasma flight.

Your memories are part of the world saved on this device and travel with its export. Shared multiplayer astral travel is not included in this release.
